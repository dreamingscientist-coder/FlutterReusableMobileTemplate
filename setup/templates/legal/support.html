Frequently asked questions

I bought Premium but still see ads

Open Settings → Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store, not by us:

  • iPhone / iPad: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Android: Google Play refund help

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open Settings → Privacy choices in the app (shown in regions where it is required). You can also reset your advertising ID in your device settings.
